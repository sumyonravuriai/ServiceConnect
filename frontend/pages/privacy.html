Legal Compliance & DPDP Act 2023

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective Immediately

At ServiceConnect, we are committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of our customers, independent service professionals, and site visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data processing practices in full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 and international data privacy benchmarks.

1. Personal Information We Collect

We only collect personal information strictly required to facilitate local on-demand service discovery, booking coordination, and platform security:

  • Customer Identifiers: Legal full name, verified email address, mobile contact number, and encrypted password credentials (hashed with BCrypt; plain passwords are never stored or logged).
  • Service Doorstep Address: Physical street address, landmark, apartment number, and postal city necessary for technician dispatch.
  • Service Provider Verification Data: Legal business trade name, trade category, professional experience, operating coverage radius, government identity credentials, and professional bio.
  • Booking & Communication Records: Appointment schedules, service descriptions, problem notes, transactional audit records, and in-app customer-provider arrival chat logs.

2. Purpose & Legal Basis of Data Processing

Your personal data is processed solely for the following explicit and lawful purposes:

  • Authenticating user sessions and validating role permissions using RFC 7519 HMAC-SHA256 JWT tokens.
  • Transmitting transactional email confirmations, verification links, and time-sensitive OTP security tokens via SMTP.
  • Connecting customers with chosen local service providers to schedule, confirm, and complete appointments.
  • Facilitating verified review submissions following completed service appointments.
  • Preventing duplicate bookings, fraud, abusive account activity, and maintaining platform security audits.

3. Data Confidentiality & Disclosure Rules

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to third-party advertisers.

Data is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis as follows:

  • Assigned Service Provider: When a booking is confirmed, only the assigned professional receives the customer's doorstep address, contact phone number, and problem description.
  • Administrative Review: ServiceConnect compliance officers access provider registration documents solely to verify trade qualifications prior to public listing approval.
  • Legal Mandates: Disclosures made solely where compelled by applicable law, court order, or governmental regulatory authority.

4. Data Storage & Security Safeguards

We implement industry-standard cryptographic and administrative safeguards:

  • Passwords are irreversibly hashed using standard BCrypt with high-cost salt factors.
  • All network communications between the browser client and Spring Boot REST APIs occur over TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • SQL injection defenses are enforced via parameterized Spring JDBC prepared statements.
  • Tokens are strictly time-bounded (email verification OTPs expire in 24 hours, password reset tokens expire in 1 hour).

5. Your Privacy Rights

Under the DPDP Act and applicable privacy statutes, you hold the following statutory rights:

  • Right to Access: View all personal data, address books, and appointment histories recorded in your dashboard.
  • Right to Rectification: Update outdated phone numbers, service areas, or profile information at any time.
  • Right to Erasure: Request permanent deletion of your account and personal identifiers from our production database by emailing our Data Protection Officer.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Submit inquiries or complaints regarding data handling to our Grievance Officer.
